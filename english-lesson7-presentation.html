Reading Comprehension

Read, find the information, then answer.

📖
Daniel is 13 years old and lives near his school. Every morning, he walks to school with his sister. His favorite subject is Science because he likes learning how things work. After school, Daniel usually goes home, eats lunch and finishes his homework. Twice a week, he plays basketball with his friends. In the evening, he sometimes reads a book or watches TV. He usually goes to bed at 10:00.
1. How old is Daniel?
A. 12 · B. 13 · C. 14
2. How does Daniel go to school?
A. By bus · B. By car · C. He walks
3. What is Daniel’s favorite subject?
A. Science · B. English · C. History
4. Why does he like Science?
A. Because it is easy · B. Because he likes learning how things work · C. Because his friends like it
5. What does Daniel do twice a week?
A. Plays basketball · B. Reads a book · C. Watches TV
6. What time does he usually go to bed?
A. 9:00 · B. 10:00 · C. 11:00
