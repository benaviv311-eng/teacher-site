PART 3 · TASK

Reading Comprehension

Details + meaning + inference

📖
Last month, Maya's class started a project to reduce plastic waste at school. They noticed that many students bought water bottles every day and threw them away after one use. Maya suggested placing refill stations near the gym and cafeteria, but the school could not afford new machines. Instead, the class designed posters and asked students to bring reusable bottles. During the first week, only a few students changed their habits. The class did not give up. They visited other classes, explained why the project mattered, and created a competition between grades. After a month, the school collected almost half as many plastic bottles as before. Maya was pleased, but she said the project was not finished because changing a habit takes time.
1. What problem did the class notice?
A. Students disliked water · B. Many students threw away plastic bottles every day · C. The cafeteria stopped selling drinks
2. Why didn't they install refill stations?
A. Students refused · B. No room · C. The school could not afford the machines
3. What did they do instead?
A. Posters + reusable bottles · B. Ban water · C. Buy machines themselves
4. What happened in the first week?
A. Most changed immediately · B. Only a few changed · C. The project ended
5. Why create a competition?
A. To win a prize · B. To sell bottles · C. To encourage more students to change
6. “Almost half as many” means…
A. More bottles · B. The number fell to about half · C. Exactly the same
7. What can we infer from Maya's final comment?
A. The project failed · B. Machines are the only solution · C. Lasting change needs time and continued effort
Question 7 requires inference.5 / 10
PART 3 · ANSWERS

Reading Comprehension

Find the evidence.

✅
The class wanted to reduce plastic waste. Refill stations were not possible because the school could not afford new machines. They used posters, reusable bottles, class visits and a competition. After a month, the school collected almost half as many plastic bottles as before. Maya's final comment shows that lasting change needs time and continued effort.
