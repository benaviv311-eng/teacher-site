3. Context Challenge

1. I didn't eat breakfast, so by 10:00 I was very ______. hungry / thirsty / tired / cold
2. We need a knife because we want to ______ the watermelon. cut / cook / order / carry
3. The restaurant is full, so we may have to ______ for a table. wait / choose / serve / taste
4. Tom ordered a large pizza, but after two pieces he was already ______. full / hungry / thirsty / fresh
5. Sara chose the salad instead of the burger because she wanted something ______. lighter / sweeter / hotter / larger

6. Daniel has 30 shekels. A sandwich costs 22 and a drink costs 12. He wants both, but he doesn't have enough money. He needs to ______ his order.
change / finish / taste / serve
