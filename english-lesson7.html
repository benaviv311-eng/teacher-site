Complete the Sentences

08:00–16:00

👨‍🏫 מה אני עושה

מציג בנק של 10 מילים. כל מילה נכנסת פעם אחת בלבד. כדי לענות צריך להבין את המשפט, לא רק לזהות מילה מוכרת.

borrow | quiet | arrive | difficult | library | choose | carry | homework | remember | finish
  1. I usually ______ at school before 8:00.
  2. Please be ______ when the teacher is speaking.
  3. I sometimes ______ a book from the school library.
  4. This question is very ______, but I will try.
  5. We go to the ______ when we need a book.
  6. You can ______ one game to play with your group.
  7. I ______ my school bag every morning.
  8. I do my ______ after I come home.
  9. Try to ______ the new words for the test.
  10. I want to ______ my work before the lesson ends.
✅ תשובות למורה
1. arrive · 2. quiet · 3. borrow · 4. difficult · 5. library · 6. choose · 7. carry · 8. homework · 9. remember · 10. finish

👥 מה התלמידים עושים

3

Reading Comprehension

16:00–26:00

👨‍🏫 מה אני עושה

Daniel is 13 years old and lives near his school. Every morning, he walks to school with his sister. His favorite subject is Science because he likes learning how things work. After school, Daniel usually goes home, eats lunch and finishes his homework. Twice a week, he plays basketball with his friends. In the evening, he sometimes reads a book or watches TV. He usually goes to bed at 10:00.
  1. How old is Daniel?
    a. 12    b. 13    c. 14
  2. How does Daniel go to school?
    a. By bus    b. By car    c. He walks
  3. What is Daniel’s favorite subject?
    a. Science    b. English    c. History
  4. Why does he like Science?
    a. Because it is easy    b. Because he likes learning how things work    c. Because his friends like it
  5. What does Daniel do twice a week?
    a. Plays basketball    b. Reads a book    c. Watches TV
  6. What time does he usually go to bed?
    a. 9:00    b. 10:00    c. 11:00
✅ תשובות למורה
1-B · 2-C · 3-A · 4-B · 5-A · 6-B
