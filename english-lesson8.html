Reading Challenge 1 — Easy

LEVEL 1
Maya is 12 years old. She lives near a small park. Every morning, she walks her dog, Max, before school. Max is brown and very friendly. After school, Maya does her homework and then plays with Max in the garden. On Fridays, they walk to the park together.
1. How old is Maya?
A. 11   B. 12   C. 13
2. What is the dog's name?
A. Max   B. Tom   C. Leo
3. What color is Max?
A. White   B. Black   C. Brown
4. What does Maya do first after school?
A. Plays with Max   B. Does homework   C. Sleeps
5. Where do Maya and Max go on Fridays?
A. The zoo   B. The park   C. The school
✅ TEACHER KEY
1-B · 2-A · 3-C · 4-B · 5-B

Reading Challenge 2 — Medium

LEVEL 2
On Saturday, Tom visited the city zoo with his older brother. They arrived at 10:00. Tom wanted to see the lions first, but his brother wanted to visit the monkeys. They decided to see the monkeys before lunch and the lions after lunch. At noon, they ate sandwiches near the elephant area. Suddenly, an elephant sprayed water with its trunk, and Tom's brother got wet. Tom laughed, but then he gave his brother his jacket because the wind was cold. Before going home, they bought a postcard with a giraffe on it.
1. Who went to the zoo with Tom?
A. His friend   B. His older brother   C. His teacher
2. Which animals did they see before lunch?
A. Monkeys   B. Lions   C. Giraffes
3. Where did they eat lunch?
A. Near the lions   B. Near the elephants   C. Near the gate
4. Why did Tom give his brother his jacket?
A. He was wet and the wind was cold   B. He was hungry   C. He lost his shirt
5. What happened first?
A. They saw the lions   B. They ate lunch   C. They visited the monkeys
6. What animal was on the postcard?
A. A giraffe   B. A monkey   C. An elephant
✅ TEACHER KEY
1-B · 2-A · 3-B · 4-A · 5-C · 6-A

Reading Challenge 3 — Challenge

LEVEL 3
Last month, students in Class 7B started a project to help a local animal shelter. At first, they planned to collect only food, but after speaking with a shelter worker, they learned that the shelter also needed blankets, toys, and people to walk the dogs. The class divided into three teams. One team made posters for the school, another collected supplies, and the third created a weekend volunteer schedule. During the first week, only a few students brought donations, so the class changed its plan and visited other classrooms to explain why the shelter needed help. By the end of the month, they had collected more than expected, and twelve students had volunteered at the shelter. Their teacher said the most important result was not the number of donations but the fact that the students saw a problem and worked together to solve it.
1. What was the original plan?
A. Collect only food   B. Walk dogs every day   C. Make posters
2. What did they learn from the shelter worker?
A. The shelter was closed   B. The shelter needed more than food   C. The dogs were dangerous
3. Why did the class change its plan?
A. They had too many donations   B. Few students donated at first   C. The teacher stopped the project
4. What can we understand from the classroom visits?
A. Explaining the problem helped more people join   B. Students disliked posters   C. The shelter asked for money
5. Which sentence is NOT true?
A. Twelve students volunteered   B. The class worked in teams   C. The teacher said donations were the only important result
6. What is the main idea?
A. Zoos are better than shelters   B. Teamwork and changing a plan can solve a problem   C. Posters are the best school activity
✅ TEACHER KEY
1-A · 2-B · 3-B · 4-A · 5-C · 6-B
