4

Context Challenge — Hard

18:00–26:00

👨‍🏫 מה אני עושה

בכל שאלה יש 4 אפשרויות שנראות הגיוניות. רק הבנת כל ההקשר מאפשרת לבחור נכון.

CONTEXT CHALLENGE

1. I didn't eat breakfast, so by 10:00 I was very ______.
hungry / thirsty / tired / cold

2. We need a knife because we want to ______ the watermelon.
cut / cook / order / carry

3. The restaurant is full, so we may have to ______ for a table.
wait / choose / serve / taste

4. Tom ordered a large pizza, but after two pieces he was already ______.
full / hungry / thirsty / fresh

5. Sara chose the salad instead of the burger because she wanted something ______.
lighter / sweeter / hotter / larger
HARD MODE

6. Daniel has 30 shekels. A sandwich costs 22 and a drink costs 12. He wants both, but he doesn't have enough money. He needs to ______ his order.
change / finish / taste / serve

7. Maya tasted one spoon of soup and immediately drank a large glass of water. The soup was probably very ______.
spicy / cold / cheap / healthy

8. Noa ate breakfast at 7:00 and a sandwich at 9:00. At 10:00 she says, “I don't want any food.” Noa is probably ______.
full / thirsty / hungry / tired
✅ תשובות למורה
1 hungry · 2 cut · 3 wait · 4 full · 5 lighter · 6 change · 7 spicy · 8 full

👥 מה התלמידים עושים

קוראים את כל הסיטואציה, פוסלים מסיחים ובוחרים את המילה המדויקת ביותר.
