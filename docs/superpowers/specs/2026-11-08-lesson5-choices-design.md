# Lesson 5: Classroom choice activities — Design

## Intent

Add to the **existing** Lesson 5 slide deck two optional exercises, without replacing any of the approved 24 teaching slides:

1. A private, notebook-based 15-question reflection on Cooler / Closer / Cleaner, with a balanced, shuffled answer key and a mixed-profile option.
2. Five additional **oral** class questions with three plausible responses each. Students vote aloud before the next slide reveals which Grover approach each answer exemplifies, its strength, and a possible tradeoff.

## Scope and safety

- All questions are original, inspired by Tim Grover's motivational framework, not a validated personality assessment. Never assign immutable labels or rank children.
- Student slides reveal no mapping before the private quiz is complete, and the five oral questions follow the quiz to avoid priming.
- Append to the existing slide sequence; use the existing visual system and teacher-controller synchronization.
- Keep the original 50-minute route intact. The quiz (~20 minutes) or oral discussion (8–12 minutes) are alternate/extension activities, not automatically extra time in a 50-minute class.
- Source data for both activities is shared by the projector and the teacher's answer guide to avoid mismatches.

## Design decision

The user chose a projected presentation with notebook scoring over phone-based individual tests, then clarified that both activities belong inside the **same existing presentation**. We therefore append 31 slides (1 introduction, 15 questions, 3 key screens, 2 reflections, 10 oral question/reveal screens) to the original 24, making 55 total.
